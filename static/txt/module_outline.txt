---

label: 1.1.1
title: Definition
content: 
At its simplest, a data center is just a building that houses technology infrastructure such as servers,
data storage systems, networking devices, and operating systems. Previously, companies would own a
data center to facilitate their computing needs. Nowadays, cloud service providers (CSPs) own massive
data centers capable of fulfilling the needs for multiple separate companies at once.
