The reason for the massive power bill is because of both the servers running (which is what runs things like ChatGPT) and cooling systems for the aforementioned servers. --- label: 2.1.2 title: How Much Energy is Needed? content: . --- label: 2.1.3 title: Where Does the Energy Come From? content: Power Usage Effectiveness (PUE) is how we are able to tell how efficiently a data center is using energy. It is calculated by dividing the total energy cost of a data center by the energy cost of IT equipment (such as servers). The best possible PUE a data center can have is 1.0 (the lower the better), but most data centers average ~1.5 PUE or, in special cases, 1.2 PUE for very efficient data centers. What this means is that if a data center has a PUE score of 1.5 and its servers alone consume the same amount of power as 100,000 homes, the entire building is actually using the same amount of power as 150,000 homes. In fact, Meta currently has plans for a data center, and when finished, it will use twice as much energy as the entire city of New Orleans! Another data center planned in Wyoming will use more power than every home in the state combined. --- label: 2.1.4 title: Understanding Power Usage Effectiveness (PUE) content: In 2025, the energy sources for data centers were as follows:
