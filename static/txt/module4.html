--- label: 4.1 title: Geographical Effects --- label: 4.1.1 title: Interactive map of data centers content:
In this map, you can see data centers that are currently in operation in the US. You can also see data centers which are under construction or being planned for the future.

Data centers are springing up all over the country, and many of them are bigger than existing data centers currently in operation. This means that they have a bigger environmental impact, need more power to operate, and take up more water as well.
--- label: 4.2 title: Good Habits --- label: 4.2.1 title: Less AI usage content: Because of how demanding using AI is for data centers, cutting back on its usage as much as possible will greatly reduce the energy and water spent during your time online. One way to do this is to disable the AI overview that automatically pops up when you search on Google. After making a search, there will be a dropdown menu that says “More”. By clicking on this, you are able to set your search to the “Web” setting which will disable future AI overviews, reducing your energy cost to ~4% of what it would’ve been if AI was included. --- label: 4.2.2 title: Petition your representatives content: On a larger scale, you are always able to find your representative at https://www.house.gov/representatives/find-your-representative in order to push for a change in legislation! To create a real discussion, it is important to draft a formal statement regarding what you would like to change and gather signatures to show support for your cause.
