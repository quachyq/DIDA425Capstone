1.2 – Bones of a Data Center


Services like Netflix use data centers to deliver already existing content to users. These systems are highly efficient, and are considered to be low power systems. Services such as OpenAI’s ChatGPT, however, use a lot more power and resources because they are generative.

ChatGPT and other large language models such as Google Gemini, Meta AI, and others do not deliver existing data files, they are used to create new content. Many of these services now offer image and or video creation. This requires the model to use lots of energy.

In 2024, data centers accounted for about 4.4% of total electricity consumption in the US, and that number is expected to grow to anywhere from 6.7 to 12% by 2028.
